About Me

My research examines how context and differences in human interpretation shape language understanding, with a focus on improving the reliability of NLP evaluation. Drawing on linguistic analysis and human judgments, I study variation in language data, explanation-based evaluation, and information access across languages and regional varieties. From April 2023 to March 2026, I was a postdoctoral researcher in MaiNLP (Munich AI & NLP) at LMU Munich, working with Barbara Plank. I received my Ph.D. in Linguistics from Georgetown University, where I studied discourse structure and multilingual language resources with Amir Zeldes and Nathan Schneider.
